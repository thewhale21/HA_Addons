Charge now

Plug in now for this long.
More
The schedule is adjusted the same way as for auto plug-in: the charge is added as a one-off slot (shown in the schedule below, where you can change it) that unplugs at the first ready time your supplier accepts after these hours, and the ready time is set to it. If it meets a scheduled slot they become one. With Force schedule on supplier on, if it would take a day over the daily limit (Settings › Your supplier), you're asked first, and the next scheduled slot then starts just late enough to stay within it (and later ones too, if skipping it isn't enough).

Schedule On

Plugs in and unplugs at set times, so your supplier can charge then.
More
Each slot runs from a plug-in to its unplug, every week on the days you pick, or once on a date. Times are in your Home Assistant time zone. A time missed while the add-on is stopped isn't run later; if it starts inside a slot, it plugs in straight away. Unplugging during a session ends it. Two slots that meet (e.g. 06:00–12:00 and 12:00–18:00) unplug and plug straight back in where they meet, so each is its own charge with its own ready time (Octopus: 6 hours before a 12:00 reset and 6 after). With Force schedule on, a slot longer than the daily limit across its reset time is split there the same way, as is a charge from auto plug-in, the car plugged in sensor or Charge now. Skip a slot to stop it running once. A no-charging time (add one the same way, choosing No-charging time) keeps the schedule out of, for example, your off-peak hours: slots can't go in it, and auto plug-in, the car plugged in sensor and Charge now unplug for it and plug back in after, still ending when they would have.
The last 3 days as they happened, then the next 7. Click a slot to change, delete or skip it, or click or drag across a day to add one, or a no-charging time.

Auto re-plug

Unplugs and plugs back in if your supplier doesn't start a session.
More
Octopus sometimes doesn't start a session after you plug in. If there's still no session after the minutes below, the add-on unplugs for the time below and plugs back in, up to the number of tries below, then gives up until the car is next unplugged. A slot your supplier has planned for later counts as an answer, so it waits for that; during a slot that's running, it waits until 10 minutes after the next hour or half hour.
