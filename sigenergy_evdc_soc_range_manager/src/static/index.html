WhenWhat and whyLimitsSoC
Car battery: usable capacity
—
Each dot is one charge or discharge that moved the SoC far enough: kWh at the charger ÷ SoC moved. Filled dots are charges, rings are discharges. Read the trend more than any one dot: it includes the car's own charging losses, and the SoC is only whole numbers.
Round-trip losses: last 30 days
—
From the SoC: the kWh it takes to put 1% into the car against the kWh you get back for 1% out, from each charge and discharge that moves the SoC far enough (the median of the last 10 of each). That's the charger and the car; the inverter's own AC ↔ DC loss is on top (see Is it paying?).
Is it paying? Last 30 days
—
Charging costs the import rate when importing, otherwise the export rate you gave up; discharging is worth the import rate it saves, or the export rate when exporting. Prices from your rate sensors (Settings), the inverter's AC ↔ DC efficiency from Settings › Statistics.
Discharge dropouts
—
Times the car stopped discharging without being told to (not a stop from here, not unplugging). A sudden rise can mean a change in the car or its software.
