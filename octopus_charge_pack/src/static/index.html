Your home battery

SoC sensor
Its state of charge (%), so the History tab can show whether each session really charged or discharged it.
More
For each completed session, its SoC at the start and the end is read back from Home Assistant's history. Moving 2% or more the right way counts. Earlier sessions are checked when you pick it, as far back as Home Assistant keeps history (10 days by default).

Calendars

Keeps two Home Assistant calendars, one of the planned charges and one of the planned discharges, for HA's calendar and calendar triggers (e.g. 15 minutes before a discharge).
More
Local Calendars called Octopus Charge Pack Charge (calendar.octopus_charge_pack_charge) and Octopus Charge Pack Discharge (calendar.octopus_charge_pack_discharge), made the first time. Charges and discharges still to come are added as Octopus plans them, and taken out if Octopus drops them before they start; ones that have started stay, as a history. Idle dispatches aren't added. Only the add-on's own events are changed, so you can add yours. Turning this off leaves the calendar as it is.
